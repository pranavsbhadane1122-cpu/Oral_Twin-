WHAT THE MODEL ACTUALLY LEARNED
-------------------------------
The failures are not random - they are cavity-shaped. Three measurements
confirm what the overlays suggest, so this is not an impression:

  1. Dice(predicted LESION mask, ground-truth CAVITY mask) = 0.494.
     The predicted lesion mask resembles the oral cavity MORE than it
     resembles the lesion it is supposed to find (0.362).

  2. Pearson r(per-image lesion Dice, lesion area / cavity area) = 0.777
     over 340 test images. Lesion Dice is largely a function of how much of
     the mouth the lesion happens to fill.

  3. Lesion Dice stratified by that ratio:
        lesion covers   0- 10% of cavity   n=144   Dice 0.201
        lesion covers  10- 25% of cavity   n= 53   Dice 0.520
        lesion covers  25- 50% of cavity   n= 62   Dice 0.690
        lesion covers  50- 75% of cavity   n= 67   Dice 0.766
        lesion covers  75-101% of cavity   n= 14   Dice 0.778

  4. On the 109 Healthy test images, where no lesion is annotated at all, the
     model still paints a mean 15.5% of the frame as lesion, and only 3 of
     109 (2.8%) predict essentially nothing.

The lesion head has learned "outline the mouth", not "find the lesion". It
scores well precisely on the images where outlining the mouth is close to the
right answer - the whole-lip and whole-tongue annotations - and collapses on
the small focal lesions, which are the clinically interesting ones and the
majority of the set (144 of 340 cover under 10% of the cavity).

This is the same species of defect as the Phase 2 Grad-CAM failure: a
plausible aggregate number resting on the model having found an easier
correlated target than the one asked for. The headline 0.362 is not simply
"a weak segmenter"; it is a cavity segmenter being scored on a lesion task.

Train lesion Dice (0.364) and validation lesion Dice (0.347) are essentially
equal, so this is not overfitting. The model is not failing to generalise
what it learned - it learned the wrong thing, consistently, on both splits.
More epochs, more regularisation or more augmentation do not address that.

GATE OUTCOME
------------
  numeric: lesion Dice 0.362 vs 0.70 required   FAIL
  visual:  5 of 10 vs 8 of 10 required          FAIL

Both halves fail. Per the Phase 3 brief, work stops here. No tuning, no
threshold adjustment, no re-run with different hyperparameters.

The cavity channel, for the record, is genuinely good: Dice 0.933
[0.926, 0.940], IoU 0.883 [0.872, 0.893]. If anything here is reusable it is
the cavity segmenter, not the lesion one.

OralTwin is a screening aid, not a diagnostic tool.
