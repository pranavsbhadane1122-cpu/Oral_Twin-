SPOT-CHECK: is the cavity model usable for "how much of the frame is mouth"
on MIO images? Judged by looking at 10 renders, not inferred from Dice.
==========================================================================

Per-image estimates (visit1 of 10 randomly chosen pairs, seed 20251007):

  pair        class        model   heuristic   usable?
  pair_0004   calculus     37.1%      59.2%    NO
  pair_0010   calculus     39.9%      59.2%    NO
  pair_0026   caries       89.2%      96.2%    yes
  pair_0066   caries       74.3%      89.3%    yes
  pair_0070   caries       99.7%     100.0%    yes
  pair_0088   gingivitis   95.4%     100.0%    yes
  pair_0119   caries       74.3%     100.0%    yes (understates)
  pair_0129   caries       75.8%     100.0%    yes
  pair_0133   caries       34.1%      97.2%    yes
  pair_0148   gingivitis   22.0%      69.5%    NO

VERDICT: 7 of 10 sensible, 3 of 10 badly wrong - and NOT usable as a number.

What the model does well. On views that resemble its training data - a face
with a visible mouth aperture (pair_0133) - it traces the opening accurately,
and 34% is the correct answer there. On full-frame intraoral photographs
(pair_0026, 0070, 0088) it correctly reports that nearly the whole frame is
mouth. On pair_0129 it correctly EXCLUDES a journal figure caption bar burned
into the bottom of the image. That is better behaviour than expected.

Where it fails, and why it matters. The three failures are one coherent class:
extreme close-ups of a tooth-and-gum strip (pair_0004, 0010, 0148), where the
entire frame is already inside the mouth. There the model outlines only the
soft tissue, excluding the teeth, and reports 22-40% when the true answer is
essentially 100%.

That is the worst possible direction for this error. A caption reading "the
mouth occupies 22% of this photograph" invites the user to move CLOSER, when
they are already too close. The number would not merely be wrong, it would
give advice that makes the next photograph worse.

The obvious fallback is also unusable. The HSV heuristic returns exactly
100.0% on 4 of these 10 images. That is not a measurement - it is the
heuristic's "my mask looked implausible, use the whole frame" path, which is
indistinguishable in the output from a genuine full-frame intraoral view. A
figure that silently reports a failure as 100% is worse than one that is
merely noisy.

DECISION, which departs from the brief and is flagged as such.

The brief said: if the cavity masks are not usable, use the HSV heuristic for
that figure instead. The measurement says the heuristic is WORSE for this
specific figure, so substituting it would be following the letter of the
instruction into a worse outcome.

Instead, the caption layer:
  - does NOT print a mouth-fraction percentage when the two estimates
    disagree by more than caption.mouth_fraction_tolerance. It says the
    extent could not be measured reliably, and says why.
  - prints the fraction only when both independent sources agree, which is
    the only condition under which either is evidence.
  - treats "is an intraoral view present at all" as a separate and much
    easier question, which both sources answer correctly on all 10 images.
    That judgement is safe to make; the precise fraction is not.

Both estimates are kept in the structured output for inspection. Only the
user-facing sentence is withheld.
